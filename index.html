Frequently asked questions

What partners want to know

What does Daraksha Overseas LLP do?

We act as a B2B sourcing partner for manpower agencies and foreign employers, managing candidates from sourcing through flight dispatch.

Which candidates can you supply?

White-collar engineering, IT, corporate, healthcare and hospitality professionals; blue-collar construction and infrastructure trades; and professional drivers.

How are candidates verified?

Through GAMCA-approved medical laboratories, professional skill testing centres and additional licence and road testing for drivers.

How quickly will we receive candidates?

Initial candidate shortlists are delivered within 48 hours of requirement posting.

Does the process cover legal and travel formalities?

Yes. The workflow covers employment contracts, salary structures, visas, briefing, ticketing, PoE clearance and airport dispatch.

Who will we work with?

Designated Partners and the CGM oversee operations, supported by HR, logistics, dispatch, documentation and quality teams.
